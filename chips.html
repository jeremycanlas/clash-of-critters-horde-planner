Chips

Fan-made, unofficial. Tatari data and sprites from the Clash of Critters Wiki. The chips themselves are not on the wiki at all. Every name and every line of text here was read off the in-game Chip Gallery by hand, so any of it can be wrong. Check it in game if it matters. Chips are offered at random during a run and you keep three, so nothing here is a loadout you can choose; it is a way to know what an offer is worth before you take it.
