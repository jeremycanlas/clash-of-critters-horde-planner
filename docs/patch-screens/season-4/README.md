# Season 4 screenshots

The in-game Season Update screen and Chip Gallery for Season 4, forwarded by Jacc
from the official Clash of Critters Discord on 11 Nov 2026 (originally posted 17:00)
into The Horde Homies #horde-formations-and-help. `data/changes.json` (the Season 4
preview) was read off these by hand.

Named `s4-<message>-<image>.jpg`, in the order they were posted:

- `s4-01-*`: the Horde Invasion buff list (portraits only)
- `s4-02-*`, `s4-03-*`: Chip Gallery, one new chip's tooltip open in each
- `s4-04-*` to `s4-12-*`: one skill card each, with its "Buffed This Season: old → new" tag

## CHIPS (tooltips in Chip Gallery; new or changed this season? to confirm)
Never Too Late: Upgrade your lowest-level deployed Tatari to Lvl 8 after 12 waves.
Last Stand: You and your teammate's Tatari gain a 30% DMG Boost, but the battle ends immediately if a Zobo reaches the bottom of the map.
Special Emblem III: Your Specialist Tatari gain 16% ATK, DEF, and Max HP.
Support Emblem III: Your Support Tatari gain 20% DEF and 28% Max HP.
Healer Emblem III: Your Healer Tatari gain 24% ATK and Max HP.
DPS Emblem III: Your DPS Tatari gain 40% ATK.
Guard Emblem III: Your Guardian Tatari gain 24% ATK and DEF.
Tank Emblem III: Your Tank Tatari gain 32% DEF and 20% Max HP.
Growth Spurt: Your Tatari at Lvl 5 or below gain 30% ATK, DEF, and Max HP.
Back at It: Whenever your Tatari is knocked down, they will auto-revive at the cost of energizers equal to 80% of their redeployment requirement. Revival requires having sufficient energizers.
New Frontiers: The deployment area is expanded forward by 3 rows. Both you and your teammate can deploy or move Tatari into the new area.
Delayed Gains: Obtain 400 energizers after 5 waves.
