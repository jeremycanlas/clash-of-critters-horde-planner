# Discord guide draft: Horde Drafter

Bullet points to paste into the official Discord, one section per message
(Discord caps a message at 2,000 characters). Links point at the live site, so
the Skill data and Community-filter links only work once this branch is pushed.

Site: https://jeremycanlas.github.io/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/

---

## 1. What it is

- **Horde Drafter** is a free fan-made planner for Horde Invasion, Gold Rush and Arena formations
- Plan your board before you spend resources, then share it as a picture or a link
- Works on phone and computer, no account needed. Your formations stay in your browser unless you post one

## 2. Build a formation (phone)

- Tap **Roster**, tap a Tatari, then tap a square on the field
- Tap a chip under the field to move one; double-tap to take it off
- Pick the mode at the top: **Solo**, **Co-op** or **Gold Rush** (Gold Rush is the 5 × 5 field, same as Arena)
- **Plan** lists who to level first; **Summary** shows what the board adds up to
- **Flex slots**: mark a square as "anyone's choice" when you haven't settled it

## 3. Co-op Horde

- Horde is always co-op in the game. Use **Co-op** to plan both sides, or **Solo** to plan just yours
- To plan with your partner live: **Formation ▾ → Live → Start a live session**, send them the link, and you both edit the same board
- Switch between your side and theirs with the **P1 / P2** tabs above the field

## 4. Share and post

- **Share** (bottom bar on a phone) gives you the card as a picture, a link, or straight into Discord with your phone's share sheet
- **Post to Community** is at the bottom of Share. Sign in with Discord once, and your build goes in the public gallery
- Browse builds: https://jeremycanlas.github.io/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/community.html
  - Gold Rush only: …/community.html?mode=goldrush
  - Co-op only: …/community.html?mode=coop

## 5. Skill data (numbers no other site has)

- Every Tatari's skill as the in-game panel shows it: Damage Factor, Cooldown, each effect, and the **Arena Factor**
- **Arena Factor** only matters in Gold Rush and Arena: a skill's damage against another Tatari is multiplied by it
- Each line's Horde Invasion skills (Lv 3, 5, 7) sit under its row; the whole line learns the same ones
- Link straight to one Tatari: …/skills.html#cribking
- Example: "Cribking's Hydro Claw hits for 130% every 4.5s, but only 90% against Tatari in Arena"

## 6. Also on the site

- **Patch notes**: what each update changed, Tatari by Tatari
- **Chips** and **Cozy Farm** reward tables
- **Record a range**: help fill in attack ranges the wiki doesn't have
