## Reply 1: build a formation

<!-- message -->
## 1️⃣ Build a formation in a minute
**Problem:** you want to try a board before spending resources on it.

**On a phone:**
- Tap **Roster**, tap a Tatari, then tap a square on the field
- Tap the chips under the field to move one you've brought, double-tap to take it off
- Pick your game at the top: **Solo**, **Co-op** or **Gold Rush** (Gold Rush is the 5 × 5 board, same as Arena)

**Then use the two tabs most people skip:**
- **Plan** = your level-up order. Add steps like "Silversear to Lv 3, then these three to Lv 7", so you (or your partner) know who to feed first
- **Summary** = everything the board brings: types, roles, every heal, buff and debuff, and at which level it unlocks. Tap an effect to see who brings it

**Not sure about a square yet?** Turn on **Flex slots** and mark it "anyone's choice".

**Saved** keeps your formations in your browser. Nothing leaves your phone unless you post it.

## Reply 2: co-op, live

<!-- message -->
## 2️⃣ Co-op Horde, live with your partner
**Problem:** planning co-op over chat is "no, move YOUR Frostluna one left" for 20 minutes.

- Switch to **Co-op**. The **P1 / P2** tabs pick whose side you're placing on
- Open **Formation ▾ → Live → Start a live session**
- Send your partner the link. You both edit **the same board** and see each other's moves as they happen
- No account needed, works phone to computer

When you're done, one of you hits **Share** and you both have the final board.

## Reply 3: share and Community

<!-- message -->
## 3️⃣ Share it here
**Problem:** a screenshot of the whole page is hard to read, and nobody can edit it.

Tap **Share** (bottom right on a phone):
- **Grid only** = just the field and what it brings. Reads at a glance in chat
- **Everything** = adds both benches and the level-up plan
- **Download PNG**, **Copy image** or **Copy link**. A link opens your **exact board, ready to edit**. Way better than a screenshot when someone wants to tweak it

**Post to Community** (bottom of Share) puts your build in the public gallery. Sign in with Discord once.

**Browse what others run:** https://jeremycanlas.github.io/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/community.html
Filter by **Co-op**, **Gold Rush** or tier, then **Open** any build to load it into your drafter and make it yours.

## Reply 4: Arena Factor

<!-- message -->
## 4️⃣ Arena Factor: why your skills hit softer in Gold Rush & Arena
When a skill hits **another Tatari**, its damage is multiplied by its **Arena Factor**. Horde Invasion isn't affected: against Zobos you get full damage.

The game shows it at the very bottom of each skill panel, where almost nobody looks. From the numbers read off the game, 66 lines:
- The middle line keeps **50%**. **41 of 66** keep half or less
- Only the **Glowfly** line hits *harder* (**150%**). **Zaplet** keeps 100%
- **Clucky** and **Cheerling** keep just **20%**
- So a DPS that tops your Horde board can fall flat in Arena. Check before you invest

Every line's factor is in the **Skill data** list: https://jeremycanlas.github.io/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/skills.html

## Reply 5: Horde Invasion skills

<!-- message -->
## 5️⃣ Horde Invasion skills: Lv 3, 5, 7
In Horde, each Tatari learns **three extra skills** as it levels during the run, at **Lv 3, 5 and 7**. The whole evolution line shares them, so a T1 and its T4 learn the same three. That's why the level-up order on **Plan** matters.

The biggest single hits at Lv 7:
- **Voltkit** line: Bolt Slash, **600%**
- **Tindercub** line: Flame Fists, **580%**
- **Joeyo** line: Super Launch!, **525% × 7 hits**

All 66 lines, with their numbers, on **Skill data**. Link one straight: https://jeremycanlas.github.io/clash-of-critters-horde-planner/skills.html#glowfly

## Reply 6: quick tips

<!-- message -->
## 6️⃣ Quick tips most people never find
- **Search by animal or nickname.** Type `dolphin`, `possum`, `corgi` or `poop bug` in the Roster or on Skill data. It also takes type, role, tier and skill words: `water healer`, `t4 stun`
- **Just the grid** hides everything but the field, so a normal screenshot comes out clean
- **Export / Import** (Formation ▾) saves a formation as a file, handy for moving to another device
- **Patch notes** shows every buff and nerf, Tatari by Tatari, and the drafter marks the ones that changed
- **Light or dark**: the sun button, top right

That's everything. If this helped, a 👍 on the first post helps it reach more trainers. And tell me what you'd want the site to do next 👇

## Updating the pictures

When the site changes and a picture goes out of date:

1. `node tools/guide-images.mjs` then `node tools/guide-shots.mjs 2` (the next number)
2. Commit and push; wait for the site to deploy
3. Edit each reply: change `-v1.png` to `-v2.png`. Discord fetches the new picture

A new number rather than the same file overwritten, because Discord keeps
showing the picture it fetched first. A reply can also simply be deleted and
posted again: its own reactions are lost, the opening post's are not.
