Attack range is only documented as in-game screenshots, and reach for heals, buffs and debuffs is not documented at all. Both have to be read off the game by hand, which is why most of the roster has neither. This page turns that reading into a few clicks: pick a Tatari from the roster, say where it was standing, click what it reached, and it hands you the entry to send in.

Stand it where the screenshot had it. Tiles are recorded as offsets from its own tile, so a Tatari that reaches five rows ahead has to be standing far enough back for you to click them all. The i on a roster card opens its detail sheet, which carries the wiki's range diagram when one exists — handy to have open beside this.
