Attack range is only documented as in-game screenshots, and reach for heals, buffs and debuffs is not documented at all. Both have to be read off the game by hand, which is why most of the roster has neither. This page turns that reading into a few clicks: pick a Tatari, say where it was standing, click what it reached, and it hands you the entry to send in.
