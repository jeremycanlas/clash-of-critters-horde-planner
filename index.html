        Saving, posting and finding formations

        1. Save this formation keeps what is on the field (both benches and the level-up plan with it) in this browser. Nothing leaves the page. Find your saved formations under Saved: the tab on the right edge on a computer, the button on the bottom bar on a phone.
        2. Post to Community publishes one. It is at the bottom of the Share sheet, and on each row of your Saved list. Either way it saves a copy here first, so a post is always something you still have.
        3. Posting asks you to sign in with Discord once, so the formation has a name on it and so you can delete it later. You are brought straight back to where you were.
        4. You land on your formation in Community formations as soon as it posts, at an address you can copy and send. To find it again later, open that page and pick Yours.

        Only posting sends anything anywhere, and only when you press the button that says so. Everything else here (the field, the plan, your saved formations) stays in this browser.
